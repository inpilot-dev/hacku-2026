# Manual shopping comparison and HKT demo rehearsal

The app opens on Groceries unless the URL explicitly selects another tab (`#buy`, `#wallet`, `#profile`).
Rehearse using a fresh disposable sandbox. Confirm the allowance, prepare one Wellcome basket from the
observed snapshot, show payment sources/conditions, complete the sandbox checkout, and show the receipt
and remaining budget. Then submit a blocked-category basket and show the actual wallet refusal and rule.
Use Activity & safety in `?classic` for revocation, concurrent spending and audit verification.

All cards and FPS adapters are local simulations. Wellcome cart sync is separate from payment.
The general web search has a documented provider-verification blocker; it is not the primary demo.
Routes with unverified fees are manual sandbox choices, not lowest-cost recommendations.

## Measure, do not invent

Use the same items, quantities, merchant, collection method and completion boundary for both tasks.
Have a teammate do each task and record elapsed time with a stopwatch, actions and errors. Label the
manual task on the live retailer and the agent task on an observed catalog sandbox as different systems;
neither task should submit a real order. Stop both at reviewed basket/checkout readiness. Report initial
allowance setup separately, plus whether model selection or a manual/scripted fallback was used.
Keep price observations and timestamps for both tasks; do not claim live price parity from a snapshot.
